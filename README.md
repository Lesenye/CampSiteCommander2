# CampSiteCommander2
Hi, and welcome to Campsite Commander.

It is an app thatstops you from forgetting your gear. There is literally nothing worse than driving two hours to a campsite just to realize you forgot your tent poles or your phone charger.It is super easy to use, not complicated at all, and lets you check off your stuff before you walk out the door.

Here are a few steps on how to use the app:
First:
Download the Campsite Commander from any app store of your choice and when you are  done locate it on your home screen mobile or descktop.
Pess app icon to OPen the app.

After opening the app you will see the Campsite Commander app logo, wait for it to load up and you will be automatically taken to the user intereface screen.


<img width="553" height="839" alt="Screenshot 2026-06-10 142427" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/63adf47b-0a29-4483-a1ac-abc9829a47d8" />

This is the home screen of the app.

Users are more welcome to add their Items, Categories and Quantity of their products.

After adding it to the textable area, users can then click "ADD GEAR" to recieve their products in a well organised place and also be reminded by the app about what they must not forget on their way out.

The "RESET" button can be used by users to cancel all the products they have added ina sigle click instead of having to do it manually.

The "EXIT" Button can be used to fully exit the app if users have used it or want to locate to othert apps.
