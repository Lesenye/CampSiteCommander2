# CampSiteCommander2
Hi, and welcome to Campsite Commander.

It is an app thatstops you from forgetting your gear. There is literally nothing worse than driving two hours to a campsite just to realize you forgot your tent poles or your phone charger.It is super easy to use, not complicated at all, and lets you check off your stuff before you walk out the door.
